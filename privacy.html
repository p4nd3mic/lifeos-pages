LifeOS privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026.

LifeOS is a personal app used only by its owner. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

Google data

With the owner's permission, LifeOS reads the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar using read-only access (gmail.readonly and calendar.events.readonly). It cannot send, delete or change any email or event.

What it reads is used only to answer the owner's own questions and to show him his own information. It is kept on the owner's Mac. When he asks about his mail or calendar, the relevant text is passed to the AI assistant he uses through his own subscription (Claude or ChatGPT) to write the answer for him. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

The access token is stored in a private file on the owner's Mac. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

LifeOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
